AIScanner ("the app", "we", "us") is developed by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}. This Privacy Policy explains what data the app handles and how we protect your privacy.

1.Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information, device identifiers, location data, or usage data. There is no analytics SDK, crash reporter, or telemetry of any kind.

2.How your scans are processed

When you scan a document with AIScanner:

  1. The camera capture is handled by Google Play Services' ML Kit Document Scanner activity, which runs in its own process. The resulting image is returned to AIScanner and stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  2. Optical character recognition (OCR) is performed on-device using Google ML Kit. Recognized text is stored alongside the scan, on your device.
  3. Generated PDFs and JPGs are saved to the app's private storage on your device.

At no point does AIScanner send your scans, OCR text, PDFs, or any other content to us or to any third party.

3.Network access

AIScanner does not make any outbound network requests for its core features. The app does not access the internet to function.

Google Play Services may make its own network requests for module updates (for example, downloading additional ML Kit OCR language models). These requests are made by Play Services, not by AIScanner, and are governed by Google's own privacy policy.

4.Permissions

AIScanner declares zero runtime permissions in its Android manifest:

  • The camera is accessed inside Google Play Services' scanner activity, which manages its own camera permission.
  • File imports use the Storage Access Framework, which uses per-file URIs and does not require broad storage permissions.
  • File exports use the Android FileProvider system, which does not require storage permissions.

5.Third-party SDKs

AIScanner bundles the following SDKs, all of which run on-device:

  • Google ML Kit Text Recognition (google_mlkit_text_recognition) — performs on-device OCR. No data is sent to Google for OCR.
  • Google ML Kit Document Scanner (google_mlkit_document_scanner) — Play Services UI for capturing documents.

We do not include any analytics SDK, crash reporting SDK, advertising SDK, telemetry, or tracking library.

6.Children's privacy

AIScanner is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7.Data deletion

Because the app collects no data, there is nothing for us to delete on our end. To delete your scans and app data from your device:

  • Open AIScanner and delete individual documents or folders from the app, or
  • Open Android Settings → Apps → AIScanner → Storage → Clear data, or
  • Uninstall the app from your device.

8.Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we do, we will post the updated version at the same URL where you found this policy and update the "Last updated" date above.
